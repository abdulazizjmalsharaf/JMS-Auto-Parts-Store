# JMS — Right Part. Right Price.

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** The right replacement part should not cost more than it needs to.
- **Identity:** JMS orange `#FF6B00`, engine-bay charcoal `#111827`, and hot white; Oswald drives impact words while Cairo and Inter carry Arabic and English.
- **Hero:** Real, unbranded automotive parts—brake pad, air filter, battery, and spark plug—treated as precision hardware rather than catalogue thumbnails.
- **Motion:** Hard impact cuts, a four-part orbital product pass, and a gear-to-letterform graphic match.
- **Arc:** A blunt price warning accelerates into a catalogue burst, pauses for a clear value comparison, then resolves into a confident brand lockup and final call.
- **Payoff:** The four parts collapse into one orange gear aperture around the JMS wordmark; the CTA field snaps back to the opener's black frame for a clean loop.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, 15.00 seconds.

## Shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–01.90 / 1.90s — “DON'T OVERPAY.”

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll with a direct, recognizable repair-cost problem.

**Exact content and holds:** At 00.18s a 90ms white flash reveals `CAR PART BROKEN?` in English and `قطعتك خربانة؟` in Arabic. At 00.55s both lines shear apart and are replaced by the larger orange/white `DON'T OVERPAY!` / `لا تدفع أكثر!`, held legibly until 01.61s. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Near-black full bleed. Copy owns the left 68% of the frame, with an oversized slash-shaped orange price mark cutting through the right third. The slash is a blade-like typographic interruption, not decoration. Thin speed lines live only in the far background. White flash is a single restrained accent, not repeated strobing.

**Timed choreography:** 00.00–00.18s: black with a small orange tick at center. 00.18–00.28s: one fast white flash. 00.28–00.55s: first bilingual line punches in at two staggered impacts. 00.55–00.74s: the orange slash cuts across and the first line shears away. 00.74–01.08s: the main warning scales up from 0.82 to 1.0 with a stiff overshoot. 01.08–01.55s: letter tracking clamps tighter and the slash makes a short second pass. 01.55–01.90s: slash opens into a segmented orange ring, carrying the next shot's radial aperture.

**Transition contract:** The orange ring occupies the same center and orientation as the incoming product-stage gear aperture. Combine the scale-through ring with a three-panel split; the opening battery appears already in motion through the aperture.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG type, custom radial gear ring, deterministic speed lines, clip-path panels; no external footage.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental electro-percussion bed; a sharp transient at the first flash and another at the main warning, to be aligned after listening/measuring the generated bed.

### Shot 02 / 01.90–05.80 / 3.90s — “FOUR ORIGINS. ONE PLACE.”

**Purpose:** Make the range of common replacement parts feel tangible, international, and fast to find.

**Exact content and holds:** At 02.08s `BRAKES / فحمات` with 🇺🇸; at 02.85s `FILTER / فلتر` with 🇯🇵; at 03.62s `BATTERY / بطارية` with 🇩🇪; at 04.39s `SPARK PLUG / بواجي` with 🇨🇳. Each label holds through its product's turn, then the full line `AMERICAN · JAPANESE · CHINESE · GERMAN` / `أمريكي · ياباني · صيني · ألماني` resolves from 05.02–05.70s. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The opener's ring becomes a deep graphite parts carousel. Each realistic, unbranded part fills roughly 65% of the frame's short edge in an intentionally different crop/angle: brake pad at left macro scale; pleated filter in the upper-right with radial reveal; battery front three-quarter at center; spark plug in a close diagonal. A fine orange orbit line threads the parts and becomes the origin-marker track. Labels own a lower safe strip; each part has its own quadrant/scale rather than four identical product cards.

**Timed choreography:** 01.90–02.25s: ring aperture pulls back; battery is already present at 1.25 scale and settles while a second split panel reveals the brake pad. 02.25–02.95s: hard cut to brake-pad macro; it tilts 4 degrees as the origin flag clicks onto its orbit. 02.95–03.70s: radial iris reveals pleated filter; its center pulls back into view as the label snaps in. 03.70–04.45s: battery pushes through a narrow split, settles square, and gets a brief light sweep. 04.45–05.15s: spark plug rotates a few degrees; ceramic-insulator close-up cuts to full angled object; a bright electrode glint punctuates. 05.15–05.68s: all four parts arrange on a diagonal orbit as the bilingual origin line resolves sequentially. 05.68–05.80s: orbit compresses to one orange point at the upper-right of the next price ticket.

**Transition contract:** The spark-plug electrode glint becomes the next shot's orange price marker. Combine a close push into that point with a split-fold reveal of the giant price ticket.

**Assets and technique:** Generated photoreal, unbranded transparent PNG cutouts at `public/images/`; thin SVG orbit, flags as Unicode origin markers, hard cuts and radial/split masks. No generated typography inside imagery.

**Sound intent:** The four part arrivals should land on four crisp rhythmic accents; the final electrode glint on the shot boundary, pending track measurement.

### Shot 03 / 05.80–10.15 / 4.35s — “HIGH QUALITY. LOWER PRICE.”

**Purpose:** Communicate value and confidence without inventing a currency amount, discount, or specific warranty duration.

**Exact content and holds:** At 06.04s `MARKET PRICE` / `سعر السوق` appears in white on a dark paper strip. At 06.70s the orange JMS ticket drops in bearing `JMS PRICE` / `سعر JMS`; the market strip is crossed out at 06.98s. At 07.35s `HIGH QUALITY. LOWER THAN MARKET.` / `جودة عالية.. وسعر أرخص من السوق` settles. At 08.30s a small circular seal stamps `QUALITY CHECK` / `جودة مضمونة` and `WARRANTY` / `ضمان`, with no duration or unsupported conditions. Hold the resolved comparison through 09.95s. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The product stage's orange point expands into a giant clipped ticket corner that occupies the right 55% and extends past the frame. The market strip is a narrow left-side label; its strike-through is the only horizontal motion. The orange ticket drops vertically with a paper-thick edge and a short rebound, framing the strongest hierarchy. A metallic, gear-toothed seal stamps across the ticket edge.

**Timed choreography:** 05.80–06.08s: orange point expands as the ticket edge folds toward camera; market strip is visible from the opening frame. 06.08–06.68s: market text types in via a two-beat masked reveal. 06.68–07.10s: JMS ticket drops from just above frame to a settled position; on impact, market copy is crossed through and the ticket rebounds once. 07.10–08.12s: bilingual benefit line reveals word-by-word, staying in its own left-lower zone. 08.12–08.92s: seal rotates in 12 degrees, stamps at 08.42s, then settles flat; one thin radial tick passes behind it. 08.92–09.80s: ticket and seal hold while the orange corner expands, starting the next gear-shaped aperture handoff. 09.80–10.15s: aperture opens through the seal's exact center.

**Transition contract:** Seal and next-shot gear share center, tooth count, and rotation. The seal grows through the lens while its flat lettering dissolves; the dark background stays in place as a new metallic gear takes its contour.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG paper ticket and cross-out, animated price-type treatment in Oswald, seal built from SVG and typography, no numeric price or fabricated promotion.

**Sound intent:** Low, dry ticket impact at 07.0s and a metallic stamp accent at 08.6s; align cuts to measured music onsets.

### Shot 04 / 10.15–13.05 / 2.90s — “JMS.”

**Purpose:** Turn the range and value claims into one memorable brand signature.

**Exact content and holds:** At 10.50s `JMS` appears in white. At 11.25s the slogan resolves below: `RIGHT PART. RIGHT PRICE.` / `قطعة صح.. بسعر صح`. Keep both clear through 12.87s. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** The badge's toothed circle becomes a brushed-metal gear, centered and large enough to frame the wordmark. JMS is plain typeset lettering rather than a fabricated official logo, with a small orange tooth at the gear's upper-right. The gear's inner negative space forms a mechanical aperture behind the letters.

**Timed choreography:** 10.15–10.57s: the incoming aperture slows from a fast spin and reveals the full gear's machined grooves. 10.57–10.97s: the gear's outer teeth lock with a two-step mechanical settle. 10.87–11.29s: the white JMS letters draw in on a single horizontal axis, then land with a modest compression. 11.29–11.87s: the bilingual slogan unmasks below in opposite directions (English left-to-right, Arabic right-to-left). 11.87–12.70s: gear rotates only 8 degrees and comes to rest; a restrained orange highlight travels one tooth segment. 12.70–13.05s: the whole lockup compresses horizontally into an orange wedge pointed toward the CTA's center.

**Transition contract:** The orange wedge is the exact leading edge of the next orange CTA field; it unfolds across the screen as type lands, rather than fading to a new card.

**Assets and technique:** Plain JMS wordmark typography, custom SVG gear outline with machined grooves, mask reveal; no unofficial external logo.

**Sound intent:** A short mechanical settle supports the logo lockup; a clean beat carries the wedge handoff.

### Shot 05 / 13.05–15.00 / 1.95s — “SHOP NOW.”

**Purpose:** Close on a simple action and create a loopable end-to-start handoff.

**Exact content and holds:** At 13.25s `SHOP NOW AT JMS` / `تسوّق الآن على JMS` lands in white on orange. Keep legible through 14.62s. The final 0.38s is an intentional moving shutter, not an empty hold. No narration.

**Composition and artistic idea:** A vivid orange panel fills the frame—an intentional palette reversal from the charcoal brand card. CTA is large, centered, and typeset as film copy, not an interactive button. Four unbranded product cutouts skim around the outer edge without crossing the text, then converge as small dark silhouettes into a central gear aperture.

**Timed choreography:** 13.05–13.27s: wedge from the logo expands into a full orange plane with a two-panel unfold. 13.27–13.69s: the CTA slams into center with a crisp shadowless impact; Arabic and English settle as one bilingual lockup. 13.69–14.22s: four product silhouettes travel around the outside perimeter and converge into an outline gear; one compact pulse hits at 14.16s. 14.22–14.62s: the CTA holds; silhouettes snap into the center. 14.62–15.00s: a gear-toothed shutter closes over the orange field to black; the shutter's last orange sliver matches the opener's center tick at 00.00s.

**Transition contract:** Final black and center tick line up to the opening frame. The cut is carried by the same orange aperture in reverse, with a short panel shutter and a hard cut on black.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG orange shutter, reused four transparent product cutouts as small perimeter silhouettes, typography. No navigation or interactive controls inside the film.

**Sound intent:** Final rhythmic impact at 14.6s, followed by a short clean tail compatible with the loop.
